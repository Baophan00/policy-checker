PolicyChecker
GenLayer StudioContract ↗

Check policy compliance

Enter a question, a policy document URL, and an expected answer. GenLayer validators will check if the policy supports or contradicts.

How it works: The contract fetches the policy via gl.nondet.web.render, asks an LLM to determine if it supports/contradicts/is silent on the question, and requires validator consensus (prompt_comparative) on the verdict before committing.
