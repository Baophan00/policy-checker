4. Working arrangements

4.1 Office hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours.

4.2 Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with prior manager approval. Requests are made in the HR portal at least one working day in advance and are approved per week.

Remote work is not a substitute for childcare. Employees working remotely must be available for video calls during core hours.

4.3 Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance of 300 EUR.

7. Travel

Business travel is reimbursed at cost with receipts. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours.
